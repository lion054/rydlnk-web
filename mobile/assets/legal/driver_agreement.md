## 1. Independent basis
You provide transportation as an independent party, not as an employee of
Rydlnk, unless the law provides otherwise. [Classification must be confirmed by
counsel for your jurisdiction.]

## 2. Verification (required before you drive)
Before accepting trips you must submit and keep current:
- Government ID
- Valid driver's licence
- Vehicle registration
- **Valid insurance** covering passenger transport
You may not accept trips until Rydlnk approves your documents. Expired documents
suspend your ability to drive.

## 3. Your obligations
- Maintain a roadworthy, insured vehicle.
- Drive lawfully and safely; no intoxication or reckless conduct.
- Pick up all pooled riders on the trip and follow the stop sequence.
- Treat riders with respect; no discrimination or harassment.

## 4. Fares & earnings
You earn the trip fare less any platform fee, paid out per the payout schedule.
[Finalise fee %, payout timing, taxes with your finance/legal setup.]

## 5. Insurance & liability
You are responsible for maintaining insurance valid for carrying passengers. You
are responsible for your own conduct and vehicle. [Set out indemnity and the
interaction with any platform cover, with counsel and your insurer.]

## 6. Background checks
You consent to identity and background checks via our vetting provider as a
condition of approval and continued access. [Contract a checks vendor.]

## 7. Suspension & removal
We may suspend or remove you for safety concerns, low ratings, expired documents,
or breach of these terms or the Terms of Service.

## 8. Data
Your name, vehicle and rating are shown to riders on your trips. Your live
location is shared with those riders while a trip is active.
