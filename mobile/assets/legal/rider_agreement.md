# Rider Agreement

**DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Must be reviewed and finalised by counsel before
use.

_Last updated: [DATE] · Version 0.1-draft_

## 1. Shared rides
Rydlnk is a **pooling** service. Your ride may be shared with other riders on a
similar route, and your fare is **split** between the riders on the trip. The
per-person fare can change as riders join or leave.

## 2. Booking & cancellation
- You may book recurring or one-time rides and cancel upcoming rides.
- Cancelling releases your seat; co-riders keep theirs.
- Late cancellations may incur a fee as disclosed at booking. [Finalise policy.]

## 3. Conduct
Be ready at your pickup point and on time out of respect for co-riders and the
driver. No unlawful, unsafe or abusive behaviour. No smoking, weapons or illegal
goods.

## 4. Fares & payment
Fares are estimates based on distance and time and split across the trip's
riders. Charges are processed through our payment provider. [Finalise with your
payments setup.]

## 5. Safety
Use in-app trip-sharing and the emergency button as needed. In an emergency,
call local emergency services first. Rate your driver honestly after each trip.

## 6. Location & privacy
Your pickup and drop-off points are used to match you and are shared with the
assigned driver for pickup. See the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Liability
Rydlnk connects you with independent drivers and does not itself provide the
transport. [Lawyer to tailor liability and dispute clauses.]
