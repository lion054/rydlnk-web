# Privacy Policy

**DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Must be reviewed and finalised by counsel and
aligned with your jurisdiction's data-protection law (e.g. Zimbabwe's Data
Protection Act) before use.

_Last updated: [DATE] · Version 0.1-draft_

## 1. What we collect
- **Account**: name, email, phone, home/work areas.
- **Location**: pickup/drop-off coordinates you set; drivers' live GPS while
  online or on a trip.
- **Trips & payments**: bookings, ride history, fares, payment references
  (card data is held by our processor, not by us).
- **Driver verification**: ID, licence, vehicle registration and insurance
  documents (stored privately).
- **Device**: app diagnostics and push tokens.

## 2. Why we use it
To match and pool rides, calculate fares, enable tracking and messaging between
trip participants, verify drivers, process payments, keep the platform safe, and
meet legal obligations.

## 3. Sharing
- With the **other participants of your trip** (a driver sees riders' pickup
  points and names; riders see the assigned driver's name, vehicle and rating).
- With **service providers** (hosting, payments, maps/routing) under contract.
- With **authorities** where legally required or to protect safety.
We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. Location specifics
Drivers' live location is shared with riders **only while a trip is active**.
Riders' precise coordinates are used for matching and shown to the assigned
driver for pickup.

## 5. Retention
We keep data for as long as your account is active and as required for legal,
tax and safety purposes, then delete or anonymise it.

## 6. Your rights
Access, correction, deletion and objection, subject to local law. Contact us to
exercise these. Deleting your account removes your profile and cascades your
trips.

## 7. Security
Access is restricted by row-level security; verification documents sit in a
private bucket scoped to each driver. No system is perfectly secure.

## 8. Children
Not intended for under-18s.

## 9. Changes & contact
We will notify material changes in-app. [privacy@rydlnk.app]
